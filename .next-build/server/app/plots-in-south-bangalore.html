South Bengaluru: the questions.

Where exactly are the south Bangalore plots located?+

The register covers APC Circle (Jigani/Anekal), the Jigani industrial hub and Kanakapura Road. Each listing names its own location, and the desk will send the survey numbers and a map pin before a visit.

Are plots in Jigani approved by BMRDA?+

Layouts in the Jigani and APC Circle belt are generally sanctioned by BMRDA or the local planning authority, with RERA registration for the phase on sale. The register carries the approval type and RERA reference for each project; verify them against the documents you are asked to sign.

What is the difference between a plot on Kanakapura Road and one in Jigani?+

Kanakapura Road is a residential and villa corridor with metro and expressway access; Jigani and APC Circle are industrial-adjacent and priced for proximity to employment. The desk will show both against your budget instead of steering you to the higher-priced one.

Can I buy a resale villa in south Bangalore through the desk?+

Yes. One seller-listed five-bedroom resale at Purva Symphony, The Sound of Water, off Bannerghatta Road, is carried at a seller-quoted ₹9 crore. Configuration, area, price and availability on that unit are the seller’s claims and must be verified against the documents.
