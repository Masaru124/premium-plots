Questions buyers ask.

Approvals, brokerage, visits and Dubai remittance. Ask anything that is not covered.

What does the seller advertise for the Purva Symphony resale?+

A five-bedroom resale unit at Purva Symphony, The Sound of Water, currently quoted at ₹9.0 Cr, with 5,000 sq ft of land and 7,000 sq ft built-up area reported by the seller. Verify the sanctioned plan, measurements, price and availability against original documents before you commit.

Which corridors does the desk follow?+

The airport and STRR belt, Chikkaballapura and Nandi Hills, Bannerghatta and NICE Road, the APC Circle and Jigani industrial edge, Tumkur and Kolar, and Dubai. Growth figures shown here are corridor-level commentary, not audited returns.

How are approvals checked?+

Each project is checked for its approval record and title documents before it is published. Every listing carries the approval type and the RERA reference supplied for that project, and both should be verified against the original documents for the plot or unit you choose.

Why is there no brokerage for buyers?+

As an authorised channel partner, the desk is paid by the developer. Advisory, documentation support and site visit arrangements are free to buyers.

Can Indian residents buy in Dubai?+

Under the RBI Liberalised Remittance Scheme, Indian residents may remit up to USD 250,000 per financial year. Yields, visa eligibility and return figures quoted by sellers are indicative and must be confirmed with the developer and your advisor.
