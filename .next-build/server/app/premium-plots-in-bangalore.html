What premium means here.

Four checks run before a plot is written into the register. A layout that fails any of them is not listed, however attractive the price looks.

  • Title before price

    The mother deed, the encumbrance certificate and the survey numbers are read before a layout is listed. A plot that cannot show a clean chain of title is not carried in the register, whatever it costs per square foot.

  • The sanction, not the brochure

    Plotted layouts are checked for the sanctioning authority that has jurisdiction over the survey number — BIAAPA, BMRDA, BDA or the local town planning body — and for the RERA registration that applies to the phase being sold.

  • Khata and revenue record

    A-Khata or the applicable revenue record, tax receipts and the survey sketch are matched against the plot number being offered. Buyers are shown what exists rather than told that everything is clear.

  • Infrastructure that is actually built

    Roads, drainage, water lines, street lighting and the compound wall are inspected on site. Promised infrastructure and delivered infrastructure are described differently in the listing.

Questions buyers ask.

What counts as a premium plot in Bangalore?+

In this register, premium describes the paperwork and the layout as much as the price: a sanctioned gated plotted development with clear title, a revenue record, defined plot dimensions and delivered infrastructure. Price bands run from about ₹35 lakh in Chikkaballapura to ₹2 crore for the largest estate plots, with villa plots and villas above that.

How much does a premium plot cost in Bangalore today?+

The register currently lists plotted developments from ₹35 lakh to ₹2 crore, and villa plots up to 4,499 sq.ft at the top of that band. Rates differ sharply by corridor, so compare the per-square-foot figure for the specific layout rather than the headline starting price.

Which corridors does Setty Relators cover?+

Chikkaballapura and the Nandi Hills belt, the Airport corridor around Devanahalli and Chikkajala, the south industrial belt around APC Circle and Jigani, Kanakapura Road and Tumkur Road in Bengaluru, and Dubai Marina for overseas buyers.

Is a premium plot a better buy than an apartment?+

They behave differently. A plot is land with no construction or maintenance cost, but it produces no rent until something is built. An apartment produces rent immediately and depreciates as a building. The desk will run both against your horizon rather than recommend one category outright.

Are bank loans available for plots in Bangalore?+

Plot loans are available from national lenders for approved layouts, usually for a lower share of the cost and a shorter tenure than a home loan. The register lists the lenders named for each project; confirm eligibility for the specific plot number with the lender.

Does the desk charge buyers a brokerage?+

No. Setty Relators works as an authorised channel partner and is compensated by the developer, so buyers pay no brokerage for listings in the register. Ask for the charges that do apply — registration, stamp duty and any statutory fees — in writing.
