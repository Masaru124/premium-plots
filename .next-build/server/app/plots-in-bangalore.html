Which approvals should I check on a plot in Bangalore?+

Ask for four things: the layout sanction from the authority with jurisdiction over the survey number (BIAAPA, BMRDA, BDA or the local planning body), the RERA Karnataka registration for the phase being sold, the khata record, and the deed chain with its encumbrance certificate. The register carries the approval type stated for each project; verify it against the documents before you pay.
